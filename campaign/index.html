Campaign Planning

Build a full integrated campaign brief — including messaging framework, channel strategy, and launch calendar — from a single prompt.

🎯 What this tool does

This prompt takes a campaign idea and turns it into a structured, ready-to-execute plan. It produces a campaign brief with goals, audience targeting, messaging hierarchy, channel recommendations, and a launch timeline — the kind of document that aligns your whole team before a single asset is created.

📋 Your Prompt — Copy & Paste
Ready-to-use prompt
You are a senior B2B demand generation strategist. Build a complete integrated campaign brief based on the following inputs. CAMPAIGN CONTEXT: - Company: [Company name] - What we're promoting: [Product, feature, offer, or event] - Campaign goal: [e.g., generate 50 MQLs / drive 200 webinar registrations / accelerate pipeline] - Primary audience: [Job title, company size, industry] - Secondary audience: [If applicable — influencers, champions, economic buyers] - Campaign duration: [e.g., 6 weeks, starting [date]] - Budget range: [e.g., $10K / $50K / enterprise / unknown] - Key proof points or differentiators: [Stats, case studies, or unique claims to feature] CAMPAIGN BRIEF OUTPUT — Please structure with these sections: 1. CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW — One-paragraph summary of the campaign and its strategic role 2. CAMPAIGN THEME & HEADLINE — A unifying creative concept and headline for the campaign 3. MESSAGING HIERARCHY — Primary message, 3 supporting messages, and proof point for each 4. AUDIENCE TARGETING — Where to find them, how to segment, and any exclusions 5. CHANNEL PLAN — Recommended channels (paid, owned, earned) with rationale and role of each 6. CONTENT & ASSET LIST — The assets needed to execute (emails, ads, landing page, etc.) 7. LAUNCH CALENDAR — Week-by-week timeline from pre-launch through post-campaign follow-up 8. SUCCESS METRICS — KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) to track, with recommended targets 9. RISKS & MITIGATION — Top 2 risks and how to address them
💡 Pro tips for better results
📅 Give Claude a specific launch date. It will work backwards and build a realistic timeline — revealing what needs to start immediately.
🎯 ABM (Account-Based Marketing) campaigns? Add a list of target accounts in the audience field. Claude can tailor the channel plan to account-level tactics.
💰 Include your budget range. Claude will recommend channels appropriate to your spend level — not just aspirational ones.
📊 After the brief, ask: "Now write the creative brief for the lead magnet." or "Draft the campaign kickoff email to the internal team."
