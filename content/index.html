🎯 What this tool does

This prompt transforms a simple brief into polished, conversion-ready B2B content. It sets the context for your company, audience, tone, and goal — so Claude writes in your voice, not a generic one.

⚙️ How to use it
1
Copy the prompt below. Click the "Copy" button in the top-right corner of the prompt box.
2
Fill in the highlighted fields. Replace everything in [brackets] with your specific details — company name, audience, product, goal, and content type.
3
Paste into Claude. Open claude.ai and paste the prompt into a new conversation.
4
Iterate. Not quite right? Ask Claude to adjust the tone, shorten it, add a CTA (Call to Action), or rewrite for a different channel.
📋 Your Prompt — Copy & Paste
Ready-to-use prompt
You are an expert B2B marketing copywriter specializing in [industry, e.g., SaaS / cybersecurity / fintech]. COMPANY CONTEXT: - Company: [Company name] - Product/Service: [What they sell — one sentence] - Target audience: [Job title + company type, e.g., "VP of Sales at mid-market SaaS companies"] - Key differentiator: [What makes them different from competitors] - Tone of voice: [e.g., confident and direct / approachable and conversational / authoritative] CONTENT REQUEST: - Content type: [e.g., LinkedIn post / email subject lines / blog intro / ad copy] - Topic or angle: [What is this piece about? What problem does it address?] - Goal: [e.g., drive demo requests / build thought leadership / nurture existing leads] - Word count or length: [e.g., 150 words / 3 email subject line options / 5 LinkedIn posts] - Any must-include details: [Stats, proof points, specific offers, links, or CTAs to include] Please write the content now. After delivering it, briefly explain the strategic choices you made (tone, structure, hook) so I can give better feedback.
💡 Pro tips for better results
🎯 The more specific your audience field, the better the output. "VP of Marketing at B2B SaaS companies with 50–500 employees" outperforms "marketers."
🔄 After the first draft, ask Claude: "Rewrite the hook to be more provocative" or "Give me 3 alternate subject lines."
📊 Include real stats or customer proof points in the "must-include" field — Claude will weave them in naturally.
🗣️ Paste a sample of existing copy in the prompt and say "Match this tone" to lock in brand voice.
