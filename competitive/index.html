⚔️ Intel Tool

Competitive Intelligence

Analyze competitor positioning, surface gaps, and produce sales-ready battlecards — without spending hours on manual research.

🎯 What this tool does

This prompt generates a structured competitive battlecard — a one-page cheat sheet that helps your sales team win deals against a specific competitor. It covers positioning, strengths, weaknesses, common objections, and killer questions to ask.

Sales battlecards Positioning analysis Win/loss framing Objection handling Messaging gap analysis Competitive landscape overview
⚙️ How to use it
1
Copy the prompt using the button in the prompt box.
2
Fill in the brackets. Provide your company details and the competitor you want to analyze. The more detail you include, the more precise the output.
3
Paste into Claude. Claude will generate a full battlecard with structured sections.
4
Ask Claude to dig deeper. Try: "Add a trap question for when the prospect mentions [feature]" or "Reframe the weaknesses as opportunities."
📋 Your Prompt — Copy & Paste
Ready-to-use prompt
You are a B2B competitive intelligence analyst. Generate a complete, sales-ready battlecard for the following comparison. OUR COMPANY: - Company: [Your company name] - Product/Service: [What you sell — one sentence] - Core differentiators: [Top 2–3 things that make you unique] - Ideal customer: [Job title + company type + size] - Price positioning: [e.g., premium / mid-market / value] COMPETITOR: - Competitor name: [Competitor company name] - Their website: [URL if known] - What they're known for: [Their positioning or flagship feature] - Where they typically win: [Situations or buyer types where they beat us] - Known weaknesses: [Any known gaps, complaints, or limitations] BATTLECARD OUTPUT — Please structure your response with these exact sections: 1. COMPETITOR SNAPSHOT — 2–3 sentence summary of who they are and how they position 2. WHERE THEY WIN — 3 bullet points on when/why they beat us 3. WHERE WE WIN — 3 bullet points on when/why we beat them 4. LANDMINES — 2 "trap questions" reps can ask to expose competitor weaknesses 5. OBJECTION HANDLERS — Top 3 objections when a prospect prefers the competitor, with our rebuttal for each 6. ONE-LINE POSITIONING STATEMENT — A single sentence reps can say when asked "how are you different from [competitor]?"
💡 Pro tips for better results
🌐 Paste the competitor's homepage or pricing page copy into the prompt — Claude can analyze their own language against yours.
🔁 Run this prompt once per competitor. Then keep a folder of battlecards — one file per competitor — and update quarterly.
🗣️ Ask Claude: "Rewrite this for a sales rep who has 30 seconds to explain the difference." Great for pocket cards.
📢 Add real quotes from G2 or Gartner reviews in the "Known weaknesses" field to ground the battlecard in buyer sentiment.
